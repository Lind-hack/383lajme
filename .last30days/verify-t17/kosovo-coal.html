Members of parliament from the Alliance for the Future of Kosovo (AAK) threw coal in front of the entrance to the Kosovo Government building today, expressing dissatisfaction with the energy situation and assessing that after the energy crisis, Kosovo is also in political darkness.

Alliance MP Burim Ramadani said that over the past few years, Kosovo has moved from energy darkness to a situation he considers political darkness.

"With Kurti and Self-Determination, Kosovo has been in energy darkness for the last six or seven years, which has kept the country in that state, and now it is moving into political darkness," Ramadani said.

He said that the symbolism of coal has to do with Kosovo's underground resources, criticizing the lack of investment in the energy sector and the purchase of electricity from abroad.

"The message and symbolism of coal, Kosovo coal, is that Kosovo still rests on coal, but electricity is not produced," said Ramadani.

According to him, one of the main points of the Alliance's program is the construction of a new thermal power plant in Kosovo.

Ramadani also claimed that over the years, around a billion euros have been spent on purchasing electricity from Serbia and other countries, stating that, according to him, the "energy mafia" has profited from this situation.

The Alliance announced that symbolic activities will continue and will become more numerous and intense.
