US Court Overturns Suspension of Immigrant Visa Processing for 75 Countries, Including Kosovo and Bosnia and Herzegovina

23.08.2026 - 13:37
BGNES

The list also includes Afghanistan, Egypt, Brazil, Iraq, Nigeria, Somalia, Thailand and Yemen.

A federal judge has overturned the US administration's decision to suspend immigrant visa processing for citizens of 75 countries, including several Western Balkan states.

Judge Jeanette Vargas of the US District Court for the Southern District of New York ruled that the administration's decision was “unlawful” and that Secretary of State Marco Rubio had exceeded his authority in adopting it.

The measure, which took effect in January, suspended the processing of immigrant visa applications from citizens of 75 countries, including Kosovo, Albania, North Macedonia, Montenegro and Bosnia and Herzegovina.

The list also includes Afghanistan, Egypt, Brazil, Iraq, Nigeria, Somalia, Thailand and Yemen, according to RFE/RL.

In January, the US State Department said immigrant visa processing for citizens of the 75 countries would be suspended because immigrants from those countries receive an “unacceptably high level of welfare benefits” from US taxpayers. The measure was intended to remain in place until Washington could ensure that new arrivals would not abuse the US welfare system.

Judge Vargas ruled, however, that consular officials had been unlawfully instructed to deny immigrant visas solely on the basis of applicants' countries of origin.

The US administration can appeal the federal court's decision.

Before the measure was introduced, on January 4 President Donald Trump published a list on Truth Social of countries whose citizens living in the United States he claimed were receiving government welfare benefits.

Kosovo was among the countries listed. The post claimed that 46% of Kosovo immigrants in the US receive government welfare benefits.

During his campaign for a second term, Trump pledged to deport millions of undocumented migrants. After returning to the White House, his administration introduced a series of measures aimed at accelerating deportations and restricting illegal border crossings. | BGNES
