Kurti: In constant communication with KFOR for the full opening of the bridge over the Ibar River

2026-08-22 15:51:02, Kosova & Bota CNA

Kosovo's acting Prime Minister, Albin Kurti, said that the country's institutions are in constant communication with KFOR regarding the full opening of the main bridge over the Ibar River and its "complete takeover" by the Kosovo Police.

"We are in daily communication, both with the Prime Minister's Office and the Ministry of Internal Affairs, so that everything goes smoothly and peacefully," Kurti said during a media conference on August 22, when asked when the main bridge in Mitrovica is expected to open.

He said that on Saturday afternoon there will be a meeting with KFOR Commander Ozgan Uluta?, while describing it as "absurd" that the bridge will continue to be closed.

"We must remember that after the construction of the new bridges, it has become absurd to keep that bridge closed, and the fact that citizens have freely circulated across these new bridges and there has been no increase in either incidents or tensions, shows that the circulation of people and goods, the ever-increasing freedom of movement, is beneficial not only for the economy, but also for peace and security," Kurti stressed.

The main bridge over the Ibar is open to pedestrians, but not to vehicle traffic.

Since the end of the war, NATO's peacekeeping mission in Kosovo, KFOR, has kept the bridge, which has been a point of interethnic clashes for years, under surveillance. 

The Kosovo government had announced in July 2024 the opening of the main bridge over the Ibar River to vehicle traffic, an initiative that caused tensions and reactions from the international community.

Meanwhile, the Kosovo executive has built two more bridges near the main bridge – one for pedestrians and one for vehicles – with the aim of connecting North Mitrovica with South Mitrovica.

The main bridge over the Ibar River is considered one of the most sensitive points in terms of security in northern Kosovo./rel
