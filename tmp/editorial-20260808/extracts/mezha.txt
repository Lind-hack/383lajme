 A diplomatic visit to Belgrade brought an important reminder that Kyiv is not adjusting its position on one of Europe’s most sensitive disputes.

According to  YouTube .

President Volodymyr Zelenskyy confirmed that Ukraine’s position on Kosovo remains unchanged: Ukraine respects Serbia’s territorial sovereignty.

He made the statement while speaking with media representatives in Belgrade.

We respect the territorial integrity of our partners, and we respect international law. Therefore, our position on (…) Kosovo’s unilateral declaration of independence remains unchanged. We have always emphasized this, and Ukraine’s policy has not changed.

– Volodymyr Zelenskyy

Zelenskyy thanked the partners for respecting Ukraine’s territorial integrity and sovereignty, particularly their recognition that Crimea belongs to Ukraine.

Serbia’s position has also remained unchanged, including at the UN General Assembly, the head of state noted.

Kosovo is a partially recognized republic that unilaterally declared independence from Serbia in 2008.

Ultimately, it is important for Ukraine to uphold the principles of international law and respect for the sovereignty of partner countries in the European context.
