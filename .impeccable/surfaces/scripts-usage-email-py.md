# 383 daily activity email

Read mode. The owner needs to understand what visitors use in under a minute.
This extends the existing 383 identity: ink, cream, orange; system fonts and
inline tables keep the email readable when web fonts and scripts are unavailable.

The opening gives a factual click/time takeaway. The feature activity matrix
then compares visits, clicks, and active time with independent labelled heat
scales and exact numbers. Ranked controls, destination clicks, total active time,
page interaction, Clarity popular pages, and Clarity average active time follow.
An amber section separates frustration signals from positive engagement.

The primary action opens Clarity's on-page heatmaps. The email heatmap aggregates
feature activity; it does not pretend to show click coordinates on a screenshot.
Missing measurements have explicit empty states. New-counter coverage is shown.
Data never depends on color alone. Mobile uses smaller section padding and a
fluid table; numeric columns remain readable without horizontal scrolling.

Direction key: `383-usage-email-v2`. Code-led extension; no image comp or new
visual identity. No shipping raster assets, no decorative motion, no script.
